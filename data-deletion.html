Data Deletion Instructions

BEFAST AUTOMOTIVE TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD respects your privacy and provides a way to request deletion of information associated with our social media management application.

How to request deletion

  1. Disconnect your Facebook account or Page from the application, if you still have access.
  2. Email info@befast-auto.org with the subject “Data Deletion Request”.
  3. Include the Facebook Page name or account identifier associated with the connection. Do not send your password or access token.

We will review your request and delete applicable data from our system, subject to legal obligations and necessary security records.
