Privacy Policy

Privacy information for our social media management application

Last updated: November 26, 2026

BEFAST AUTOMOTIVE TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD (“BEFAST”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects privacy. This policy describes information handled when authorized company personnel connect and manage our business social media accounts and Pages through our social media management application.

1. Information We Access

When an authorized user connects a Facebook account or Page, the application may access information allowed by the permissions approved by that user and Meta, such as:

  • Facebook account and Page identifiers and basic profile information;
  • Page name and information needed to identify or manage the Page;
  • Content, captions, and media selected for publication;
  • Publishing and Page-management information permitted by the granted permissions; and
  • Access tokens needed to maintain an authorized connection.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information only to connect authorized accounts, manage our company's social media presence, prepare and publish business updates, promote our products and services, and maintain or troubleshoot the application.

3. Data Storage and Security

Our social media management system is self-hosted and operated by authorized personnel. Information and connection credentials may be stored in the system's database for account connection and publishing functions. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to limit access and protect stored information.

4. Sharing and Sale

We do not sell Facebook user data or share it with third parties for their independent advertising purposes. Data may be processed by infrastructure or software services necessary to operate our application. We do not use Facebook data for unrelated purposes.

5. Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Users can disconnect an account or Page from the application. To request deletion of information associated with the application, contact us at the email below. We will review the request and delete applicable data, subject to legal requirements and necessary security records.

For details on submitting a deletion request, see our Data Deletion Instructions.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for business social media management and is not directed to children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
